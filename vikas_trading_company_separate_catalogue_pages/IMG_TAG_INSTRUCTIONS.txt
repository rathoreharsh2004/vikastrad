All product images use explicit HTML <img src="..."> tags.

Example:
<img src="../assets/products/pvc-wallpaper/product-01.jpg" alt="Product photo 1">

Multiple photos:
<img src="../assets/products/pvc-wallpaper/product-01.jpg" alt="Product photo 1">
<img src="../assets/products/pvc-wallpaper/product-01-2.jpg" alt="Product photo 2">
<img src="../assets/products/pvc-wallpaper/product-01-3.jpg" alt="Product photo 3">

Replace src manually with your actual asset path and add/remove img tags as needed.
